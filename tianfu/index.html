Chengdu Tianfu Film City

01 / PROJECT RECORD

A city-scale film economy became the framework for a new cultural district.

Tianfu Film City was planned as more than a collection of studios. The project connected film production, cultural exchange, tourism, education, landscape and everyday public life into one urban system.

The project archive moves from city-scale planning and programme strategy to the later BRICS district architectural concept proposal, where the larger ambition was tested through civic buildings, ceremonial space and regional material references.

02 / PLANNING PREMISE

Film became an urban operating system, not a single-purpose campus.

The planning studies positioned Tianfu Film City as an innovative cultural production base, a culture-led tourism experience base and a platform for cultural exchange and trade.

The three roles extend the film economy beyond shooting: people learn, produce, publish, exchange, visit and stay through one connected cultural landscape.

03 / CITY STRUCTURE

Landscape, movement and clustered growth formed the urban framework.

The strategy drew on the western Sichuan forest-disk idea: water, fields, trees, compact clusters and public paths work together to create a resilient film city rather than a single-purpose campus.

  1. 01One belt

    A film and water experience belt ties together public attractions, parks and the wider landscape.

  2. 02Two axes

    An ecological park axis and a film industry axis allow landscape and production to support one another.

  3. 03Three centres

    Headquarters, studio and BRICS centres give the large district a legible set of anchors.

  4. 04Ten minutes

    Small blocks, dense routes and green corridors bring work and daily life within walking distance.

04 / PROGRAMME AND PHASING

The film economy was connected to daily public life.

The planning archive links production to learning, hospitality, public culture and landscape. The result is both a working environment and a destination with its own daily rhythms.

The project was organised as a sequence of making, meeting and experiencing.

01Make / production

Studios, digital shooting, post-production, equipment services and creative offices.

02Meet / exchange

BRICS theatre, film festival, conference centre, museum, media exchange and rights trading.

03Experience / public life

Film-themed streets, esports, digital art parks, hotels, commerce and waterfront events.

05 / ARCHITECTURAL DEEPENING

The BRICS district translated the city strategy into a civic gateway.

The later architectural scheme focuses on a district that had to represent Chengdu and China to an international audience. Its logic moves from the macro scale of national ceremony to the human scale of bamboo, lantern and Shu brocade.

  1. 01正 / Ceremony

    Order, axis, steps and portico establish a clear civic sequence.

  2. 02山 / Landscape

    Rooflines and massing respond to mountain and water rather than becoming isolated objects.

  3. 03门 / Welcome

    Layered thresholds make the district read as an open international address.

  4. 04竹 / 灯 / 锦

    Bamboo, lantern and Shu brocade translate regional culture into facade, shade and detail.
