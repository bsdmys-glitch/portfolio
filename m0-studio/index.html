Professional Practice Manual / M0 Studio

01 / PROJECT RECORD

Practice is not a chapter after design. It is a design problem in its own right.

This 73-page manual was produced for Chicago Studio in Fall 2017. It combines interviews with working architects, lecture notes on the profession and a hypothetical firm profile for M0 Studio / 本末 in Beijing.

The publication moves from how practice works to how a young office might define its services, values and references. It treats professional knowledge as material to edit, organize and make legible.

02 / EDITORIAL METHOD

The manual moves from observation to a practice position.

Rather than treating professional practice as a set of abstract rules, the publication starts with people who work inside the field. Their conversations are placed beside lectures and visual references so the student can compare values, responsibilities and everyday decisions.

The final section changes register. It proposes a hypothetical office and asks what its services, clients and design language might be.

03 / HYPOTHETICAL FIRM

M0 Studio / 本末 begins with the fundamentals.

The name 本末 describes “the fundamental and the incidental.” The imagined Beijing office works across architecture, supervision, renovation, interiors, single-family homes and preschool spaces.

Its children-focused research asks what changes when classrooms are designed around children, staff and the neighborhood rather than repeated generic rooms. A second line of work translates fashion, geometry and architectural detail into small objects and accessories.

  1. 01Architecture + nature

    Pursue the beauty of architecture and the natural world together.

  2. 02Children + neighborhood

    Design for the people who work, live and learn around a place.

  3. 03Geometry + everyday life

    Use simple forms to make daily life more legible.

04 / REFERENCE PORTFOLIO

References test the same practice position across scales.

The final section samples work by other designers instead of presenting it as project delivery. House, nursery, kindergarten, furniture and jewelry become a reference library: different scales, the same interest in proportion, material, light, play and everyday use.

These pages are labeled as references from the supplied manual, not as projects authored by M0 Studio.
