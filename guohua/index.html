02 / INDUSTRIAL MEMORY

An 18.9-hectare industrial site with a second life in the CBD.

The archive describes a former power plant beside Beijing's CBD, with intact production buildings, equipment and support facilities that retain the character of a modern thermal power station.

The 2023 China Live plan records 188,851.55 sqm of land, 111,902.94 sqm of existing building area with a 0.51 FAR, and 167,509.08 sqm of planned building area with a 0.89 FAR. It increases green coverage from 17.56% to 30.34% and grows planned parking from 323 existing spaces to 850 spaces.

03 / INDUSTRIAL SYSTEM

Four systems translate the former plant into a cultural district.

The China Live planning set groups the renewal into four connected systems: a digital technology art park, a digital creative art cluster, a national digital arts center and a green electricity route.

Together they retain the plant's rail, pipe corridors and equipment while adding public space, creative production, cultural consumption and new forms of digital performance.

  1. 01Art park

    Landscape, railway traces and industrial equipment become an open public park.

  2. 02Creative cluster

    Existing structures host studios, offices, exhibitions, leisure and offline experiences.

  3. 03Digital arts center

    The turbine hall and boiler are reorganized as a national performance and exhibition anchor.

  4. 04Green electricity route

    The pipe corridor becomes a walkable garden and the coal route becomes an industrial museum.

04 / ADAPTIVE REUSE

The office renewal uses minimal intervention as its starting point.

The main office building study identifies three design strategies: minimal intervention, low-carbon ecology and digital technology. The existing structure and material character remain legible while the envelope, public edge and building systems are updated.

The archive proposes photovoltaic panels, facade planting, a digital LED canopy, naked-eye 3D media and renewed window systems. These additions bring the office building into the wider digital culture district without erasing its original order.

The 0413 office study tests two envelope families around the retained structure. An independent external corridor strengthens the north-south connection, roof platforms create outdoor activity space, and a glazed link mediates between the existing building and new digital display surfaces. The study covers approx. 9,800 sqm total building area, including a 1,436.66 sqm ground floor.

05 / PUBLIC LIFE

New public uses are inserted through culture, commerce and shared space.

The site proposal opens the former plant through art parks, exhibition and performance spaces, creative offices, public plazas and a walkable route along the retained pipe corridor. The shared service center uses a cleared plaza and retained industrial elements to support everyday public events.

A separate Staff Home concept study is included as a secondary reference, covering dining, multipurpose and employee sports functions. It is kept outside the main project narrative, which focuses on the park planning and main office renewal.
