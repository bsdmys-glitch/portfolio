Brandeis-Asper Craft Medal

01 / PROJECT RECORD

A campus landmark becomes a small, lasting gesture.

Developed for Brandeis University's Asper Center for Global Entrepreneurship, this medal translates the memory of a place into a gift that can be held, carried and passed on.

The design turns the Samuel Lemberg Academic Center, the Squire Bridge and the School of Business and Economics into a family of architectural portraits. Each face combines site-specific imagery with the warm material language of cloisonné.

02 / START WITH THE PLACE

The medal begins with the campus, not the ornament.

Before drawing a motif, the project identifies the places that make the institution recognizable: the academic center's brick and glass, the campus bridge, the business school and its shared landscape.

These references give the object a reason to exist. The medal is not a generic award; it is a compact record of the people and places the gift is meant to honor.

03 / BUILD THE FAMILY

One institution becomes a family of site-specific faces.

The same medal grammar can hold very different campus stories. A school facade becomes a dense architectural composition; the Squire Bridge becomes a landscape drawing; the campus center becomes a portrait of movement and threshold.

The variations are designed to belong together when presented as a set, while each one remains legible as an individual gift.

04 / MAKE THE OBJECT

Cloisonné carries the design from image to material.

Fine metal wire establishes the drawing, then coloured enamel fills each compartment. The process makes architecture tactile: outlines become raised boundaries, planes become colour fields and the gold edge gives the small object a quiet weight.

The reverse is deliberately restrained. A warm gold field and the Brandeis name give the front image room to remain specific without turning the gift into a miniature billboard.

05 / DOCUMENT THE FINISHED GIFT

The final object brings the proposal into the hand.

The finished medal keeps the proposal's essential decisions intact: a vivid enamel face, a quiet gold reverse and a presentation box that gives the small object a sense of occasion.

These photographs document the delivered piece at close range, where the wire lines, granular enamel and engraved lettering become part of the experience.

06 / COMPOSE THE GIFT

The gift needs a language of its own.

The printed card introduces cloisonné as both a craft and a metaphor for leadership: shaped with vision, refined through discipline and strengthened by the courage to make something lasting.

The final presentation keeps the writing generous and the image quiet. It gives the recipient enough context to understand why this object belongs to Brandeis.
