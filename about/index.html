01 / EXPERIENCE Research, design and delivery

My earlier professional work spans urban regeneration, adaptive reuse and spatial planning. In Jieyang Ancient City, field research and historical analysis informed protection and renewal guidelines. At Mawei Shipyard, an industrial heritage framework connected existing buildings with new public uses and routes.

Beyond architecture, I led the design and delivery of a Chi Heng Foundation charity exhibition and co-founded New Minds, a member-led learning community. These projects connect research, information organization, visual communication and practical delivery.

02 / CURRENT DIRECTION Digital products and AI applications

Making everyday decisions easier.

BosGoGo is my current self-initiated product case study: a map-first experience for discovering activities in Boston. The work covers product framing, information architecture, discovery and save flows, and UI / UX design. It is at the product-concept and interface-prototype stage.

I am now exploring AI applications that help people organize information and make decisions. My focus is on connecting a specific use of AI with a clear interaction, a useful outcome and a prototype that can be tested.
